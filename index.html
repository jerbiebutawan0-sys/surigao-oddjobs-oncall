Simple and transparent

How the service is intended to work

A clear process helps both clients and workers understand the job before anyone commits.

01

Describe the job

Clients choose a service, explain the work location and scope, and share their budget and preferred schedule.

02

Find a suitable worker

The future matching system will consider skills, experience, availability, location, tools and willingness to travel.

03

Agree before work

Both parties confirm the price, duration, materials, meals, transport, cancellation terms and responsibilities in writing.

ℹ️
Safety and fair dealing matter.

Do not pay registration fees to anyone claiming to represent this website. Verify a worker's identity and references, use safe work practices, and never share sensitive personal documents in public messages.
