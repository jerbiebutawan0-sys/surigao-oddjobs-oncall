How It Works

A simple way to connect people who need work done with people ready to work.

1

Choose a Service

Clients identify the type of work they need assistance with.

2

3

Connect with Workers

Review available applicants and coordinate the work arrangement.

Our Platform Roadmap

Building a more useful local work platform step by step.

  • Phase 1: Service categories and public landing page.
  • Phase 2: Job posting and worker registration.
  • Phase 3: Applicant management and matching.
  • Phase 4: Admin dashboard and reporting.
  • Phase 5: Expanded coverage across Surigao del Norte.
